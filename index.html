Before You Ask

The six questions we get asked first

Straight answers, including the uncomfortable one at the bottom.

We already use ChatGPT. Isn’t that enough?

For drafting, summarising and research — often yes. If that turns out to be all you need, the Score will say so, and it will have cost you nothing to find out.

What a chat window does not do is sit inside your systems and finish a job unattended: read the inbox, match the invoice, update the record, escalate the exception, log what it did. That is the work we build.

We’re a 30-person business. Aren’t we too small for this?

Small is the point. Below roughly 200 people you rarely have anyone whose actual job is AI, and hiring one costs more than most of the work is worth.

The first step is sized accordingly: one working session, written back within five working days. If the numbers don’t justify a build, the report says so.

Where does our data go, and is it used to train models?

Every third party we would put in the path is named on our sub-processors page before you sign anything — not disclosed afterwards.

We build for UK/EU data residency, and the training, retention and deletion position is written into the Blueprint and the Data Processing Agreement before any of your data moves. Our standing commitment to return or delete customer data within 30 days of an engagement ending is in the privacy policy.

What if what you build doesn’t work?

That gets settled in the Blueprint, before the build. The Blueprint states what gets built, in what order, and what “working” means in numbers you agree up front.

The £3,000 is fixed, and the plan is yours whether you build it with us, in-house, or not at all. On the retainer there is a 30-day cancel notice and no annual lock-in.

Does this lock us into you?

No annual contract, and 30 days’ notice on the retainer. The Blueprint is a document you own — you can hand it to any other supplier and have them quote against it.

Where we can, we build in your stack, on your accounts, so what we built stays with you if we part ways.

You have no published client work. Why would we go first?

Correct, and we would rather say it than dress it up. QuietStaff is early. The scenarios on this site are illustrative and labelled as such, and the engineering log is the founder’s own work, not client engagements.

What we offer instead of a reference list: a first step that is free, a second step that is a fixed £3,000 and yours to take elsewhere, and a named architect doing the work rather than a delivery team you never meet.
